Sound Science

Hosted by Dr. Emily Rodriguez

About This Podcast

Discover the fascinating science behind sound and music. From acoustics to psychoacoustics, we explore how our brains process music and why certain sounds evoke specific emotions. Dr. Emily Rodriguez breaks down complex scientific concepts in an accessible way, featuring interviews with researchers and experiments you can try at home.
