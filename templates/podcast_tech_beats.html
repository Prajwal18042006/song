Tech Beats

About This Podcast

Tech Beats explores the fascinating intersection of technology and music. From AI-generated compositions to the latest music tech innovations, we dive deep into how technology is shaping the future of sound. Join host Alex Johnson as he interviews industry experts, explores cutting-edge tools, and discusses the impact of technology on music creation, distribution, and consumption.
